How SEO is kept
  • Each template replaces the design only. The live address in the second column stays exactly the same, with the same page title, description, heading and text.
  • Every page of the prototype shows, in the bar at the top, which live address it stands for.
  • The menus carry every link the live menus have today, so no page loses its internal links.
  • "New" pages are added by the online store (cart, checkout, account) or by the brief (Experience Centre).
